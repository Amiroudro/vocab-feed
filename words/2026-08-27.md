## Source set

The vocabulary was selected from [The Daily Star — “Nepal’s grief is ours too”](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/nepals-grief-ours-too-4257956), the Daily Star’s measles-outbreak editorial coverage, and relevant 27 August opinion/editorial archives from New Age, Dhaka Tribune, The Financial Express, and Prothom Alo English. The disaster, public-health, governance, and policy vocabulary was prioritised because it is useful for BCS and bank-job reading comprehension and analytical writing.
