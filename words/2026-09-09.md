## Selection and validation notes

This set intentionally prioritises **single-word vocabulary**. Only standard lexical items are included; the few noun compounds, such as *debt burden* in the source material, have been excluded from this day’s selection. The final entries are individual words, with part-of-speech labels and examples showing their newspaper-related usage.

Candidates were compared against the supplied **BCS_Bank_Vocabulary_Bank_aug15_updated.xlsx** workbook and every daily file from 30 August through 8 September. The table contains one header only, a newline after every row, exactly 35 rows, and no accidental second table. Each row includes exactly three synonyms, exactly three antonyms, exactly two collocations, a Bengali meaning, an example sentence, and a source reference.
